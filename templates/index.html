SIMPLE 3-STEP PROCESS

How to Protect Yourself from Phishing Links

Scan suspicious links before opening them to avoid credential theft and online fraud.

STEP 01

Paste Suspicious Link

Copy any unfamiliar web address from an email, SMS message, or social media post and paste it directly into the URL scanner.

STEP 02

Run Instant Security Check

Click "Scan URL" to immediately check for deceptive domain extensions, brand impersonation, and known phishing indicators.

STEP 03

Review Safety Verdict

Examine the clear threat rating and highlighted security flags to confidently decide whether the link is safe to visit.

SECURITY CAPABILITIES

Comprehensive Link Protection Features

Proactive security checks designed to identify deceptive URLs before they compromise your data.

Safe Pre-Click Inspection

Inspect destination links without visiting or connecting to the remote server, keeping your device protected from malicious downloads or tracking.

Deceptive Domain Detection

Identifies randomly generated hostnames and complex domain patterns frequently utilized by phishing campaigns to bypass filters.

Lookalike Character Defense

Detects internationalized and spoofed characters engineered to visually mimic legitimate brands and trick unsuspecting visitors.

High-Risk TLD & Shortener Shield

Flags commonly abused domain extensions and inspects disguised shortened URLs commonly distributed in phishing emails.

Credential Theft Prevention

Detects keywords and deceptive naming schemes targeted at harvesting personal accounts, banking passwords, and credentials.

Direct IP & Port Camouflage Alert

Warns you when links utilize raw numerical addresses or obscure port combinations designed to conceal the true website identity.

About PhishGuard Security

PhishGuard is a dedicated web security service focused on phishing threat identification and safer internet browsing. By evaluating link structure and threat indicators before navigation occurs, PhishGuard provides fast, accessible safety intelligence to help individuals and organizations avoid credential theft, fraudulent portals, and deceptive web schemes.
